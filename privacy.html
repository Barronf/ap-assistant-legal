Privacy Policy

AP Assistant · Ferguson CPA · Last updated: 21 August 2026

This policy explains what data AP Assistant ("the Application") accesses, why, and how it is handled. The Application is operated by Ferguson CPA ("we", "us") solely in the course of providing accounting services to our clients.

What we access

When you connect the Application to a QuickBooks Online company file, it may read and write:

  • Chart of accounts, journal entries and transaction records
  • Customer, supplier and vendor records
  • Invoices, bills, payments and receipts
  • Company profile information needed to identify the correct file

The Application accesses only the company file you explicitly authorise, and only the data needed to perform the agreed services.

Why we access it

To prepare and post accounting entries on your behalf: recording transactions, reconciling bank activity, correcting classifications and producing the records required for your financial statements.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except as set out below.
  • We do not access company files you have not authorised.

Third parties

The Application relies on Intuit's QuickBooks Online API to read and write your records; that data is handled subject to Intuit's own privacy terms. Where the Application uses automated document processing, extracted text may be transmitted to cloud infrastructure and language model providers engaged by us for that purpose. These providers act on our instructions under contractual confidentiality and data-protection terms, do not retain the transmitted content beyond what is required to return a result, and do not use it to train their models. No data is sent to any other third party.

Where processing occurs

hFerguson CPA operates in The Bahamas. The Intuit, cloud infrastructure and language model services on which the Application depends are operated outside The Bahamas, principally in the United States. By authorising the Application you consent to your records being processed in those locations under the confidentiality and data-protection terms described in this policy.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on systems controlled by Ferguson CPA and are not shared. Working copies of client records are retained only as long as needed for the engagement and are held under the same confidentiality obligations that apply to all client records in our care. Access is limited to personnel working on your engagement.If we become aware of unauthorised access to client data held by the Application, we will notify affected clients without undue delay and take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the incident.

Retention

We retain client accounting records for the period required by our professional and statutory obligations. You may request deletion of working data held by the Application at any time, subject to those obligations.

Your control

You may revoke the Application's access at any time from the Apps screen within QuickBooks Online. Revoking access ends the Application's ability to read or write your data immediately.

Confidentiality

All data accessed through the Application is client confidential information and is treated under the professional confidentiality obligations governing our practice.

Changes

We may update this policy. The version published on this page at the time of your use is the version that applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to Ferguson CPA at the contact details provided in your engagement letter.

AP Assistant is a private application used by Ferguson CPA in the course of providing accounting services. It is not distributed publicly or listed on the Intuit App Store.
